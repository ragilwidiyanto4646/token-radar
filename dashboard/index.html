Methodology — read this before trusting the board

1. Token check. A protocol counts as "no token" only if the snapshot records no gecko_id, no cmcId and no token symbol — and no variant of the same protocol (e.g. Aave V3, Hyperliquid Bridge) records one either. That matters: in the raw DefiLlama feed many versioned entries carry the parent token's symbol with a null gecko_id, and a naive gecko_id-only filter would wrongly rank Aave, Uniswap and LayerZero as token-less.

2. The watchlist is the floor, never the ceiling. The board covers the whole house watchlist (40 protocols at or above the $50M floor with no token recorded), plus the pre-token protocols below the floor the radar exists to find. Four names on the watchlist would fail our stricter screens — USDD (a stablecoin), Bittensor dTAO (a staked-asset product), Mezo Earn (an earn vault of a tokenised parent) and Saturn (a stablecoin wrapper) — but the gate requires full coverage, so they are ranked by the same model and marked ★ in the table. A protocol on the list may still have a token its DefiLlama record does not carry: the board says it records no token, never that it has none.

3. Wrapped / staked products and versioned pools excluded. Anything whose name is a wrapped or staked asset (WBTC, Binance staked ETH, Coinbase Wrapped Staked ETH…) is a product of a token that trades, not a launch candidate. Names ending in a version marker (V2, V3, 2.0…) are versioned pools of another protocol, not a launch of their own. Centralised exchanges and bridges / canonical bridges are excluded too: their TVL is other people's assets or other chains' tokens, not protocol value a launch could capitalise on.

4. Implied FDV. For each category, the median mcap / TVL of tokenised peers in the same category (≥3 peers, clamped 0.05–5×; global median otherwise) × the candidate's TVL × a growth multiplier (1 + its seven-day TVL change, clamped to −60…+120%, × 0.5) × a maturity multiplier (0.6 + min(age in days, 1095) / 1095 × 0.4; age unknown: 365 days), capped at $100B. All multiples are computed from the snapshot itself, never hard-coded. Expected value = implied FDV × launch likelihood, where likelihood is a category prior (prediction markets 0.85 … chains 0.35) adjusted for multi-chain spread, audits and age. The detail view shows every row's own derivation: TVL × multiple × growth × maturity.

5. Score (0–100) = 55 × log-normalised expected value + 30 × likelihood + 15 × log-normalised TVL. Tiers: S = top 10, then A ≥ 60, B ≥ 40, C below. Rows are ranked by implied value (rank 1 is the most valuable); the score is a second opinion, not the rank.

6. Farm plans are category playbooks (recurring real use, held positions) — every step states what it costs and how long it takes, with the capital sized from the protocol's own TVL ($50–$250) and the hold from its age (30–60 days). They describe how to be a genuine user, not a guarantee. A protocol may never launch a token, or may launch without an airdrop. Not financial advice; check audits and use only funds you can afford to lose.
